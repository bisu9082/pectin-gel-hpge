\section*{Supplementary Information}

\textbf{Low-Methoxyl Pectin--Ca$^{2+}$ Gelation as a Sample
Solidification Protocol for Marine Radioactivity Monitoring
by HPGe Gamma Spectrometry}

\section*{S1. Calculation definitions}

Paired recovery evaluates the effect of the solidification step:
\begin{equation}
R\,(\%) = \frac{A_{\mathrm{after}}}{A_{\mathrm{before}}} \times 100.
\end{equation}

Paired change is:
\begin{equation}
\Delta\,(\%) = R - 100.
\end{equation}

Bias relative to the KINS certified value is reported separately:
\begin{equation}
B\,(\%) =
\frac{A_{\mathrm{after}} - A_{\mathrm{KINS}}}{A_{\mathrm{KINS}}}
\times 100.
\end{equation}

The separation is necessary because paired recovery evaluates retention
during solidification, whereas KINS-reference bias also includes
pre-existing calibration and sample-specific differences.

\section*{S2. $^{60}$Co sensitivity analysis}

The three paired $^{60}$Co recovery values were 92.0\%, 94.9\%, and
97.4\%. The mean was 94.8\% with a sample standard deviation of 2.7
percentage points. Using Student's $t$ interval with two degrees of
freedom, the 95\% confidence interval was 88.2--101.4\%.

A descriptive Monte Carlo sensitivity analysis was performed using a
two-sided one-sample $t$-test, $\alpha=0.05$, 500000 simulations, and
random seed 20260531. Based on the observed standard deviation, the
minimum absolute shift detected with 80\% power was approximately
8.7 percentage points.

This sensitivity analysis documents the information content of the
available $n=3$ dataset. It is not a substitute for prospective
replication.
