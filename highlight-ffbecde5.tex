\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Pectin gelation fixes seawater geometry for HPGe gamma spectrometry.
    \item Controlled calcium addition yields a stable gel within two hours.
    \item Mean paired recovery is 99.9\% for $^{137}$Cs and 94.8\% for $^{60}$Co.
    \item Sixteen of 17 paired measurements remain within 80--120\% recovery.
    \item The protocol is a candidate for further shipboard validation.
\end{itemize}
